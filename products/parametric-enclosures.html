Parametric Enclosure Pack

Twenty Python scripts, each one a board-specific enclosure: ESP32 DevKit and CAM, NodeMCU, Arduino Uno and Nano, Raspberry Pi 4, 5 and Zero, STM32 Nucleo and Blue Pill, Teensy, relays, a 12 V PSU, a buck converter, sensor nodes, an OLED case, an 18650 pack and a DIN-rail box. Every dimension is a named constant.
